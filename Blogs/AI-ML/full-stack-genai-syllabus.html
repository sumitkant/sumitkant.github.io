    Use as reference, not as a project base

    • Full Stack Deep Learning LLM Bootcamp: from 2023; the ideas hold, the tools have moved on.
    • Kaggle 5-Day AI Agents Intensive: good material, but the 2026 capstone closed in July.
    • anthropics/courses on GitHub: now archived; use Anthropic Academy instead.
    • PhiloAgents (Decoding ML): one of the few free projects with a real UI, but last updated October 2025.
    • ai-engineering-hub: 90+ demo-depth projects; an idea bank rather than a capstone.

    Watchlist

    Changes from the past year that will trip up older tutorials.

    • LiteLLMVersions 1.82.7 and 1.82.8 shipped malware in a March 2026 supply-chain attack. Pin your dependency versions.
    • MCP Python SDK 2.0The built-in FastMCP class is now MCPServer; imports from mcp.server.fastmcp break. Standalone fastmcp 4.x is a separate package.
    • LLM GuardArchived July 2026 after Palo Alto Networks acquired Protect AI. Unmaintained.
    • OpenAI fine-tuningSelf-serve fine-tuning is winding down; no new jobs can be created after 6 January 2027.
    • AcquisitionsOpenAI acquired promptfoo (March 2026) and ClickHouse acquired Langfuse (January 2026). Both say they remain open source.
    • Slowing projectsChainlit is community-maintained since May 2025; Ragas and smolagents have had no release for months.
    • Moved standardsOpenTelemetry GenAI conventions now live in their own repository and are still experimental. OWASP’s 2026 LLM Top 10 replaces the 2025 list.
